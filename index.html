Final Presentation Sign-Up

Each presentation is about 10–12 minutes, including Q&A. There is a 10-minute break from 9:50 to 10:00 AM.

Presentations start promptly at 8:30 AM.

To sign up

  1. Pick an open slot in one of the two sessions below.
  2. Click Sign up next to that slot.
  3. Enter your full name and click Confirm.

To cancel or change your slot

  1. Click Cancel next to your own name.
  2. Click Yes, cancel it to confirm.
  3. To change slots, cancel first, then sign up for another open slot.

Do not cancel other people's sign-ups. Each student may hold one slot only.
